## The question

Most MCP studies use a course of several days. This prospective pilot asked what a single session does, using invasive haemodynamic monitoring in 17 patients with impaired ejection fraction before elective bypass surgery.

## The findings

Ten patients received 15 minutes of MCP before general anaesthesia and seven served as controls. The authors report an augmented stroke index (+10%) with MCP. After induction, they report systemic vascular resistance index −28%, mean arterial pressure −10% and left ventricular efficiency +22%. No complications were reported.

## The limits

With 17 patients, this is a pilot study. Its value lies in showing feasibility and the direction of the acute effect.
