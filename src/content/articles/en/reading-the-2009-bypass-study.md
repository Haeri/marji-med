## The study

The study randomised 50 patients undergoing coronary artery bypass grafting to standard postoperative care, or standard care with 30 minutes of MCP daily for the first eight postoperative days.

## The findings

Systemic vascular resistance fell by 36% with MCP and by 16% in controls. Complications occurred in one patient with MCP and seven in the control group. Postoperative hospital stay was 12.0 days with MCP and 16.8 days in controls.

## The open questions

The study was small and came from a limited number of centres. A larger multicentre trial would be needed to establish how reliably these effects on complications and hospital stay can be reproduced.
