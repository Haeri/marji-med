## Prof. Larry V. Lapanashvili

![Portrait of Prof. Larry V. Lapanashvili](/uploads/lapanashvili-portrait.jpg)

Larry V. Lapanashvili, MD, PhD, FICA, is a cardiothoracic surgeon in Tbilisi, Georgia, the inventor of muscular counterpulsation and lead author of the principal clinical studies on the method. He is an Associate Professor in Cardiovascular Physiology at New Vision University and works at the Marji Medical Center for Heart and Circulation.

## A note to colleagues

"Your independent, unbiased opinion of the method is very important to me, but it must be based on personal experience of MCP, on yourself and on your patients. If you are satisfied with the result, I am really happy. If you are disappointed, please contact me so that we can find the problem together. MCP, as one of the assisted circulation methods, unloads the heart. If you see the opposite, something is being done in the wrong way, and I will consider it an honour to help you."

Prof. Larry V. Lapanashvili
