## Treatment in Tbilisi

Marji Medical Center for Heart and Circulation invites patients with cardiovascular disease for non-invasive treatment with MCP, among other methods. Procedures are performed under the supervision of the method's inventor.

The centre holds Georgian Ministry of Health licences for outpatient care of adults (No. 000248) and children (No. 000249), issued in 2005.

## Seminars for physicians

The inventor of the method runs MCP seminars for physicians under Georgian Ministry of Health accreditation No. PS-152, "Modern Treatment Methods of Cardiovascular Pathologies".

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Duration | Three days |
| Content | Theory (the philosophy of the method) and practice |
| Certificate | Issued at the end of the seminar |
| Group size | Up to four participants |
| Fee | EUR 4,000 per group, excluding travel and accommodation |
| Location | Marji Medical Center, or by arrangement at a mountain or seaside resort |
