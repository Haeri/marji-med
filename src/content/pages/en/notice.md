## Purpose of this website

This website gives scientific and educational information on muscular counterpulsation for healthcare professionals and researchers.

## Not medical advice

Nothing here is medical advice, a diagnosis or a treatment recommendation. Patients should discuss any treatment with a qualified clinician and should not start, stop or change treatment on the basis of this website. In an emergency, contact your local emergency services.

## State of the evidence

Most published studies of muscular counterpulsation are small and come from a limited number of centres. Results are reported as published, and individual results may differ.

## Device and regulatory status

Nothing on this website is an offer to sell a device, or a claim that a device is approved for a particular use. Regulatory status differs between countries and over time. Devices referred to on this website include the CardioLa MK4 and the m.pulse. *To be supplied: the current device and its regulatory status.*

## Indications and contraindications

The indications, selection criteria and contraindications on this website are those proposed by the inventor of the method. They are not clinical guidelines, and published studies cover only some of them.

## Articles

Each article states whether it has been through journal peer review. Author publications have not, and should be read accordingly.

## Translations

This website is published in English, Russian and Georgian. If the versions differ, the English version applies.
